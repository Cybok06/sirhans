HOW TO REPORT YOUR ORDER

🔎 FIRST, CHECK YOUR ORDER STATUS!

🔴 RED NO RECEIPT BUTTON

Have you paid, but your order is not showing on the website for that particular date?
👉 After checking your order status, click the RED NO RECEIPT BUTTON to request a replacement.

🟢 GREEN REPORT BUTTON

Is your order showing DELIVERED, or have you received your receipt, but your data has not arrived after 24 hours?
👉 Click the GREEN REPORT BUTTON to report the problem.
